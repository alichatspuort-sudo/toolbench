About ToolBench

ToolBench is a collection of small, focused utility tools — a calculator, unit converter, PDF merger, image resizer, and more — built to do one job well and run entirely in your browser.

Nothing you type or upload here is sent to a server. Files you resize, PDFs you merge, and text you paste are all processed locally on your own device using your browser's built-in capabilities, then discarded the moment you leave the page.

Why we built it

Most "free tool" sites bury a simple task behind ads, sign-ups, or slow file uploads to a server. ToolBench is the opposite: open a page, do the task, get your result — no account needed.

More tools are coming

This is an active project. New tools are added over time based on what people find useful.
