Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolBench ("this site", "we") handles information when you visit.

How the tools work

Every tool on this site — the calculator, unit converter, PDF merger, image resizer, password generator, QR generator, color converter, and JSON formatter — runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Files and text you enter are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense and/or Monetag. These networks may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or the relevant network's own opt-out tools.

Analytics

We may use standard web analytics to understand overall traffic patterns, such as page views and general location by country. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
